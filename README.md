# GLaDOS: Advanced Voice Assistant Inspired by Portal
![listening](https://github.com/SmolinskiP/glados_assistant/assets/49648588/b2604584-9aeb-4d17-80a4-15899edbb3b9)


Meet **GLaDOS**, my state-of-the-art voice assistant, meticulously crafted to emulate the iconic character from the critically acclaimed game, *Portal*. This isn't just any voice assistant—it's an immersive experience that blends advanced technology with the dark humor and sharp wit that made GLaDOS a legendary figure in gaming history.

## Features

### **Authentic Voice Experience**
GLaDOS leverages a sophisticated text-to-speech module designed to faithfully reproduce the original voice from the *Portal* series. Every word spoken is a reminder of the character’s unforgettable presence, ensuring interactions that are both nostalgic and captivating.

### **Seamless Home Integration**
GLaDOS is fully integrated with my Home Assistant server via a robust API connection. This setup allows for comprehensive voice control over all smart devices in my home. Whether it’s adjusting the thermostat, turning on the lights, or managing security systems, GLaDOS handles it with the efficiency and precision you’d expect from an AI overlord.

### **Wake Word Activation**
The assistant springs into action with a specific wake word, designed to ensure quick and responsive interactions. No unnecessary delays—just immediate, efficient control over your environment, just as GLaDOS would have it.

### **Dynamic Voice-Responsive Animation**
Adding a visual layer to the experience, GLaDOS features an animation system that dynamically responds to voice input. This visual feedback not only enhances user interaction but also adds to the overall immersive experience, making GLaDOS feel more alive and, perhaps, a little more menacing.

### **Dark Humor and Sharp Wit**
What truly sets GLaDOS apart is her personality. Just like the original, this assistant is notorious for her biting sarcasm and cutting insults. Ask for a cake recipe, and you might be reminded that, “Sure, but remember, in this house, the cake is always a lie.” It’s this unique sense of humor that makes every interaction with GLaDOS both entertaining and unpredictable.

---

GLaDOS isn't just a tool—she's an experience. Whether you're a fan of *Portal* or simply looking for a voice assistant with a bit more character, GLaDOS delivers in spades. Just be prepared for a few well-placed insults along the way.
